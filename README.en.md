## What is this

A continuously updated add-on for Minecraft Bedrock. It does not change the vanilla survival rhythm — it simply makes the cobblestone lying at your feet worth picking up.

Mine stone and hunt stone creatures to stockpile stone and compressed stone, then keep upgrading your crafting stations, armor and weapons. Meanwhile the caves and the night are no longer safe: stone creatures will come for you.

> "Dig deeper, and you shall glimpse the truth."

---

## Highlights

- **Stone as currency** — every cobblestone converts into power
- **Five compression tiers** — nine stone become one; everything builds on this
- **Marrow gems** — 20+ socketable gems, each with a dedicated gear slot
- **Stone Codex** — quests, achievements and a shop in one book
- **Stone source and weekly quests** — sell coins for source, three new weeklies every game-week
- **Stone hearts** — a second health pool that regenerates out of combat
- **Stone tide** — after you beat the boss, weekend nights can turn into a siege
- **Multiple bosses** — the Stone Totem, the Tower Scion, each a different fight
- **Battle towers** — eight floors of escalating trials
- **Boulder Wasteland** — a new biome hiding relics of an older civilization
- **Localization** — zh-CN / zh-TW / en_US / ru_RU

## Getting started

1. **Mine** — cobblestone, deepslate, or stone creatures give you stone and stone nuggets.
2. **Compress** — 9 stone become one tier-1 compressed stone, 9 tier-1 become tier-2, and so on up to tier 5; tier 4 and above need the **Stone Crafting Table**.
3. **Upgrade** — turn compressed stone into tools, weapons and armor. The higher the tier, the stronger.
4. **Explore** — head into the Boulder Wasteland, Abandoned Post and Battle Tower for stone marrow, marrow gems and expansion slabs.
5. **Challenge** — gather a Blood Sacrifice Bottle and summon **the Stone Totem**. Beat it and the world remembers you: the Stone Tide begins.

Along the way keep the **Stone Codex** open — it always tells you what to do next.

## Gameplay systems

### Items

Items are the bulk of this add-on, and almost all of them are tied to stone.

- **Stone tools and weapons** — axes, pickaxes, shovels, hoes and swords are fully covered and kept up to date with new Minecraft versions; there are also hammers, spears, slingshots and a multi-tool.
- **Stone armor** — crafted from compressed stone of each tier, with stronger protection the higher you go; tier 4 and above are also made in the **Stone Crafting Table**.
- **More food** — stone bread, stone apple, stone carrot, stone melon slice… and the Stone Snickers.
- **More items** — stone nuggets, the Hidden Stone, lava remains, stone marrow, marrow gems, expansion slabs and stone coins.

> **Tip**: tier-5 compressed stone items can no longer burn, so dropping them in lava is not a disaster.

![items](https://s41.ax1x.com/2026/08/30/pnP4frd.png)

### Blocks

Here stone stops being a stepping stone and becomes a real resource.

- **Compressed stone** — five tiers, crafted level by level; the higher tiers go through the Stone Crafting Table.
- **Work stations** — the Stone Crafting Table handles high-tier crafting, the Stone Converter moves compressed stone up and down the tiers, and the Stone Forge modifies gear.
- **Ore** — stone marrow ore and deepslate stone marrow ore, rare underground minerals; Fortune works on them.
- **Mimic Stone Bloom** — a living block planted with the Stone of Rebirth; breaking a mature bloom may drop the Buried Stone.

![blocks](https://s41.ax1x.com/2026/08/29/pnPAMcD.png)

### Marrow gems

Socket a gem into gear and the gear gains its affix. **Each gem is restricted to specific slots** — put it in the wrong place and nothing happens. The table below covers only part of the set.

| Gem | Slot | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Battle Intent | Weapon | Grants Strength to you on hit |
| Blood Loss | Sword | Applies bleeding; Lv3 deals percentage max-health damage |
| Detonate | Sword | Stores 3s of damage, releases it multiplied by level |
| Chain Edge | Sword | Stacks on consecutive hits, max 25; cleared on a miss |
| Daze | Hammer | Stuns the target on hit |
| Charge | Spear | Speed while holding the spear |
| Night Vision | Helmet | Grants night vision |
| Resistance | Armor | Damage reduction; 12 levels total = Resistance V |
| Vitality | Armor | Grants health boost |
| Fire Resistance | Leggings | Grants fire resistance, duration scales with level |
| Bulwark | Armor | Chance to negate damage; Lv1 5%, full 3-piece = 50% |
| Swiftness | Boots | Grants swiftness |
| Reforge | Weapon | Restores durability every 3s, amount = level |
| Plate Mend | Armor | Restores durability every 3s, amount = level |

### Stone Codex

The **quest book, achievement list and shop** in one — the newcomer's guide and the backbone of the whole gameplay loop.

- **Chapter quests** — starting from mining and compressing, advancing chapter by chapter into exploration, combat and boss fights; progress is tracked automatically and submitted automatically, rewards are claimed in the book.
- **Achievements** — long-term goals independent of quests, claimed manually.
- **Shop** — spend **stone source** on coins, marrow gems, expansion slabs, Star Stones and other rare goods.
- **Settings** — switch how stone hearts are displayed; if the UI flickers or the action bar does not show, switch to **chat mode**.

> You can also open the Stone Codex with the `/sab` command.

### Stone coins, source and weekly quests

The stone world has an economy of its own:

- **Stone coins** — the basic currency, used to trade with the **Stone Vendor**.
- **Stone source** — a kind of "mana"; earned by selling coins, finishing weeklies and unlocking achievements in the Stone Codex, and the effective route to rare goods.
- **Weekly quests** — three random ones every game-week, with a bonus for clearing all of them.

### Stone hearts and the Stone Tide

**Stone hearts** — a second life bar independent of your health:

- Small boss **Tower Scion** drops stone hearts; eating one grants **4 stone hearts**.
- Stone hearts absorb damage first; out of combat they regenerate slowly after 5 seconds, and hunger does not affect them.
- The display mode can be switched in the Stone Codex settings (UI / chat).

**Stone Tide** — the world's "thank-you gift" after you beat the boss:

- Unlocked after your first **Stone Totem** kill.
- Every game-week's **weekend nights** have a 30% chance to break out.
- When it breaks out, large numbers of stone creatures spawn around players.
- You **cannot sleep** during a Stone Tide.

### Mobs

- [Stone Guardian](https://www.mcmod.cn/item/695533.html): a stone creature whose attacks apply Slowness.
- [Stone Archer](https://www.mcmod.cn/item/695532.html): ranged attacker that drops arrows, with sandstone and deepslate variants.
- [Stone Vendor](https://www.mcmod.cn/item/698394.html): neutral, trades in stone coins.
- **Stone Golem**: splits into Stone Guardians (nerfed — no longer summons endlessly).
- **Stone Wanderer**, **Bursting Shell**: common hostile stone creatures, the backbone of a Stone Tide.
- **Wandering Collector**: a roaming trader, its offers rebalanced several times.
- **Pillage Seeker**: belongs to the illager faction but never joins raids; roams the plains camps and attacks villagers, wandering traders, wandering collectors and you.
- **Sculk Statue**: spawns in the Deep Dark, hides among sculk blocks and summons stone golems when prey comes close.
- **Tower Scion**: mini-boss at the top of a Battle Tower; in its second phase it moves faster and gains 40% damage reduction.
- **Stone Totem**: the boss — two phases and four forms.

There are also **Mossy Guardian**, **Sandstone Guardian**, **Molten Golem** and **Ancient Stone Guardian** variants; the in-game **Bestiary** records your kills and discoveries.

![mobs](https://s41.ax1x.com/2026/08/30/pnP42xe.png)

### Structures

- **Stone Spawner**: 6 variants across different layers.
- **Abandoned Post**: risky to explore, generous in reward.
- **Pillage Seeker Camp**: pillage seekers camp in the plains.
- **Stone Fortress**: a full-scale stronghold where loot and danger come together.
- **Ancient Stone Altar**: in the Boulder Wasteland; the Sacrificial Dagger is on the upper floor, the Blood Pillar on the lower one.
- **Blood Sacrifice Fortress**: an underground fortress whose roof already carries tier-5 compressed stone and an activated Blood Pillar — summon the boss without any sacrifice.
- **Stone Battle Tower**: eight floors, with the Tower Scion guarding the top.

![structures](https://i.imgs.ovh/2026/08/30/f89a426986970139b0ce21e956b78a25.png)

### Boss: the Stone Totem

Two phases, **300 HP each**, regenerating 1 HP per second — the longer it drags on, the worse it gets.

- **Phase 1**: the shell is extremely hard and **only pickaxes deal damage**; it releases shockwaves and summons stone golems.
- **Phase 2**: teleportation unlocks, plus four forms rotating every 15 seconds:
  - **Thornmire**: evoker fangs, poison shockwave, mossy guardian summons.
  - **Warfrenzy**: quad fireballs and molten golem summons; in its last 3 seconds it roars, hitting everything within 8 blocks with rapid damage and knockback.
  - **Blight**: blindness, more sandstone guardians, attacks that cause hunger.
  - **Endmost**: dragon's breath fireballs and greatly enhanced teleportation.

**Summoning**: take the **Sacrificial Dagger** from the altar's upper floor → a glass bottle plus the dagger costs you health and yields a **Blood Vial** → craft a **False Blood Vessel** → brew it with a **Stone Heart** into a **Blood Sacrifice Bottle** → right-click a **Blood Pillar** to activate and mine it → place two **tier-5 compressed stone** blocks with the activated pillar on top, the same way you build a snow golem.

> Not in the mood for a ritual? Head to the **Blood Sacrifice Fortress** — the altar there is already built.

### Biome: Boulder Wasteland

The surface is made of cobblestone. This is where the **Ancient Stone Altar** is, and where stone marrow ore is richest — gathering the materials to summon the boss usually means spending a while here.

## Installation

1. Download the latest release from [Releases](https://github.com/CTNStudio/StoneAdventure/releases).
2. Double-click the file to import, or use Settings → Storage → Import in game.
3. Create a world and enable **both** the behavior pack and the resource pack in world settings.
4. Make sure you are on **1.26.40 or newer**, otherwise the behavior pack will fail to load.
