## What is this

A continuously updated Minecraft Bedrock add-on. It does not change the vanilla survival rhythm — it simply makes the cobblestone lying at your feet worth picking up.

Mine stone and hunt stone creatures to stockpile stone and compressed stone, then keep upgrading your crafting stations, armor and weapons. Meanwhile the caves and the night are no longer safe: stone creatures will come for you.

## Highlights

- **Stone as currency** — every cobblestone converts into power
- **Five compression tiers** — nine stone become one; everything builds on this
- **Marrow gems** — 20+ socketable gems, each with a dedicated gear slot
- **The Stone Codex** — quests, achievements and a shop in one book
- **Stone source & weekly quests** — sell coins for source, three new weeklies every game-week
- **Stone hearts** — a second health pool that regenerates out of combat
- **Stone tide** — after you beat the boss, weekend nights can turn into a siege
- **Multiple bosses** — the Stone Totem, the Tower Scion, each a different fight
- **Battle towers** — eight floors of escalating trials
- **Boulder Wasteland** — a new biome hiding relics of an older civilization
- **Fully localized** — zh-CN / zh-TW / en_US / ru_RU

## Getting started

1. **Mine** — cobblestone, deepslate, or stone creatures give you stone and stone nuggets.
2. **Compress** — 9 stone → tier 1, 9 tier 1 → tier 2, up to tier 5. Tier 4+ needs the **Stone Crafting Table**.
3. **Upgrade** — turn compressed stone into tools, weapons and armor.
4. **Explore** — Boulder Wasteland, Abandoned Post, Battle Tower: gather stone marrow, marrow gems and expansion slabs.
5. **Challenge** — brew a Blood Sacrifice Bottle and summon the **Stone Totem**. Beat it and the world remembers: the Stone Tide begins.

Keep the **Stone Codex** open — it always tells you what to do next.

## Systems

### Items
Full axe / pickaxe / shovel / hoe / sword coverage, plus hammer, spear, slingshot and multi-tool. Compressed stone armor in five tiers. Stone bread, stone apple, stone carrot, stone melon slice — and the Stone Snickers. Also stone nuggets, the Hidden Stone, lava remains, stone marrow, marrow gems, expansion slabs and stone coins.

### Blocks
Five tiers of compressed stone; Stone Crafting Table (high-tier crafting), Stone Converter (tier up/down), Stone Forge (gear modification); stone marrow ore and deepslate stone marrow ore (Fortune works); Mimic Stone Bloom, planted from the Stone of Rebirth — mature blooms may drop the Buried Stone.

### Marrow Gems
Socket a gem into gear to grant an affix. **Each gem is restricted to specific slots.**

| Gem | Slot | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Battle Intent | Weapon | Grants Strength to you on hit |
| Blood Loss | Sword | Applies bleeding; Lv3 deals percentage max-health damage |
| Detonate | Sword | Stores 3s of damage, releases it multiplied by level |
| Chain Edge | Sword | Stacks on consecutive hits, max 25; cleared on a miss |
| Daze | Hammer | Stuns the target on hit |
| Charge | Spear | Speed while holding the spear |
| Night Vision | Helmet | Grants night vision |
| Resistance | Armor | Damage reduction; 12 levels total = Resistance V |
| Vitality | Armor | Grants health boost |
| Fire Resistance | Leggings | Grants fire resistance, duration scales with level |
| Bulwark | Armor | Chance to negate damage; Lv1 5%, full 3-piece = 50% |
| Swiftness | Boots | Grants swiftness |
| Reforge | Weapon | Restores durability every 3s, amount = level |
| Plate Mend | Armor | Restores durability every 3s, amount = level |

### Stone Codex
The quest book, achievement list and shop in one. Chapter quests auto-track and auto-submit; achievements are claimed manually; the shop trades **stone source** for coins, gems, slabs and Star Stones; settings let you switch the stone-heart display between UI and chat (use chat if the action bar flickers).

### Economy
Stone coins for the Stone Vendor. Stone source from selling coins, finishing weeklies and achievements — the only route to rare goods. Three random weekly quests every game-week, with a bonus for clearing all three.

### Stone Hearts & Stone Tide
Eating a stone heart (dropped by the Tower Scion) grants **4 stone hearts** that absorb damage first; they regenerate 5 seconds after leaving combat, unaffected by hunger.

After your first Stone Totem kill, each game-week's **weekend nights** have a 30% chance of a Stone Tide: stone creatures spawn en masse around players, and you cannot sleep through it.

### Mobs
Stone Guardian (applies Slowness), Stone Archer (multiple variants, drops arrows), Stone Vendor (neutral, trades in stone coins), Stone Golem (splits into Guardians), Stone Wanderer, Bursting Shell, Wandering Collector, Pillage Seeker (illager faction, raids villages), Sculk Statue (Deep Dark ambusher), Tower Scion (mini-boss, 40% damage reduction in phase 2), and the Stone Totem boss.

### Structures
Stone Spawner (6 variants across layers), Abandoned Post, Pillage Seeker Camp, Stone Fortress, Ancient Stone Altar, Blood Sacrifice Fortress (a pre-built summoning site), and the eight-floor Battle Tower.

### Boss: Stone Totem
Two phases, 300 HP each, regenerating 1 HP per second.

- **Phase 1** — armored shell, **only pickaxes deal damage**; shockwaves and golem summons.
- **Phase 2** — teleport plus four forms rotating every 15 seconds: **Thornmire** (evoker fangs, poison shockwave, mossy guardians), **Warfrenzy** (quad fireballs, molten golems, a final 3-second roar hitting everything within 8 blocks), **Blight** (blindness, sandstone guardians, hunger), **Endmost** (dragon breath, greatly enhanced teleport).

**Summoning**: take the Sacrificial Dagger from the altar's upper floor → cut yourself for a Blood Vial → craft a False Blood Vessel → brew it with a Stone Heart into a Blood Sacrifice Bottle → right-click the Blood Pillar to activate it and mine it → place two tier-5 compressed stone blocks with the activated pillar on top (like a snow golem).

### Biome: Boulder Wasteland
Cobblestone surface, home of the Ancient Stone Altar and the richest stone marrow deposits.

## Installation

1. Download the latest `.mcaddon` from [Releases](https://github.com/CTNStudio/StoneAdventure/releases).
2. Double-click to import, or use Settings → Storage → Import in game.
3. Create a world and enable **both** the behavior pack and the resource pack.
4. Make sure you are on **1.26.60 or newer**.
